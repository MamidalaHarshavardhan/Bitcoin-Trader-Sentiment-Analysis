Bitcoin Trader Performance vs Market Sentiment - Insights

1. Traders tend to generate higher average profits during Greed market conditions compared to Fear periods.

2. Fear sentiment is associated with increased losses, indicating panic-driven trading behavior.

3. Trading activity is significantly higher during Greed phases, suggesting increased market participation during bullish conditions.

4. Buy vs Sell analysis shows that traders prefer one dominant action (based on observed distribution), reflecting market bias.

5. A subset of traders consistently perform well regardless of market sentiment, indicating advanced or strategic trading approaches.

6. Overall, market sentiment has a strong influence on both trader performance and trading behavior.
